# RemitLine synthetic salary-card entitlements

> **DEMO DATA ONLY:** These salary-card tiers are invented for the RemitLine hackathon demo. They are not real exchange-house product terms.

## Everyday Salary Card (`everyday`)

- Free domestic ATM withdrawals at participating ATMs.
- Cardless cash access up to AED 1,000 per day.
- One replacement card per calendar year at no charge.

## Salary Card Plus (`plus`)

- Free domestic ATM withdrawals at participating ATMs.
- Cardless cash access up to AED 2,000 per day.
- Two replacement cards per calendar year at no charge.

## Basic Salary Card (`basic`)

- Two free domestic ATM withdrawals each month.
- Cardless cash access up to AED 500 per day.
- Replacement card fee of AED 25.

RemitLine should report only the requested tier's factual inclusions. It must not compare tiers, recommend a tier, or provide financial advice.
